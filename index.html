What We Do

At Premier Chiropractic of Tacoma, we specialize in treating injuries resulting from automobile accidents, making us the go-to chiropractic clinic for collision victims throughout the Tacoma area. Car accidents can cause a wide range of musculoskeletal injuries, including whiplash, herniated discs, neck and back pain, and joint dysfunction — many of which may not present symptoms immediately after the incident. Our experienced chiropractors are trained to identify and treat these hidden injuries before they develop into chronic, long-term conditions. We work diligently to document your injuries thoroughly, which is also essential when filing insurance claims or pursuing personal injury cases.

As a trusted provider for L&I (Labor & Industries) claims in Washington State, Premier Chiropractic of Tacoma understands the complexities of workplace injury cases and the importance of proper documentation and treatment protocols. We are experienced in navigating the L&I system, helping injured workers receive the chiropractic care they are entitled to under Washington State law. Our team coordinates directly with L&I claim managers and other healthcare providers to ensure a streamlined, efficient recovery process. If you've been injured on the job, you can count on us to advocate for your health and help get you back to work safely and as quickly as possible.

Our personal injury chiropractic services are designed to support patients who have suffered injuries due to the negligence of others, whether in a slip-and-fall accident, a sports incident, or any other traumatic event. We work closely with personal injury attorneys and insurance companies to ensure that your medical records and treatment plans accurately reflect the extent of your injuries and the care required for recovery. This collaborative approach helps protect your legal rights while ensuring you receive the highest standard of chiropractic treatment available. Our goal is to not only help you heal physically but also to provide the documentation and support needed for a successful personal injury claim.
